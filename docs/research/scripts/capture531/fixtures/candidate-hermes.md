Findings from outside sources go on the research shelf. When you read documentation, a changelog, a paper, a benchmark or a web page and find something a later investigation of the same subject would need (a limit, a behaviour, a trade-off or a limitation of a named system), store it at once with memcue_add and shelf research, naming the source (document, version or date) in the fact. Store what the source states, not your conclusion for the task; say so when it is only a vendor's or an anonymous claim, and leave out what nothing supports. Search the shelf first (memcue_search with in shelf:research) and correct a fact that says the same or an older version. Ordinary search leaves the shelf out, and an answer with facts does not say that the shelf has more: when a task returns to a system, library or question that may have been researched before, also search with in shelf:research. A shelf fact is a lead from an earlier reading: check its source and date against the material at hand before relying on it, and correct or retire it when it no longer holds.
