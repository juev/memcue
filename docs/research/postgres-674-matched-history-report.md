The [plan projections](scripts/postgres674/results/matched-history-plans.json) retain numeric rows, loops, costs, filter removals and buffer observations from 28 `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS, FORMAT JSON)` probes; raw plans stay private. In the two current vector reference probes, correlated subplans ran 3,201/974 times and executors took 42.11/29.62 ms. At 30k they ran 12,426/3,780 times and executors took 237.09/174.17 ms. The candidate computed latest versions once, taking about 7 ms current and 30 ms at 30k, but changed join access to thousands of vector lookups and remained slower. Current reference vector-relation scan nodes reported 0.89–1.00 ms; at 30k, 4.17–5.42 ms. These rounded inclusive node times do **not** isolate cosine evaluation, which may occur higher in the plan, and cannot be summed into a CPU attribution. Query/history/join work is visible; neither “vector scan alone” nor “correlated subplan is the sole cost” is established. The measured global CTE does not fix the slowness.
